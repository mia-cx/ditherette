Two-input curves: which shape should we store?

A two-input curve reads two things from each pixel and decides one change. Here it reads hue (left to right) and lightness (bottom to top) and decides how much to boost or cut saturation. Grey in the map means no change, orange means more saturation, and blue means less.

Three ways to store that map are on the table. Try each one, run the walkthroughs, and see which one lets you say what you mean. The test chart on the right shows the result on real colours.

Hue runs left to right and wraps: the right edge meets the left. Lightness runs bottom (dark) to top (light).
