Curves work in any colour model: RGB, linear RGB, HSL, HSV, OKLab, OKLCH, CIELAB, CIELCh, or YCbCr, with checkboxes named after the model's channels. Arbitrary XY lets any channel on x adjust any channel on y, such as hue against saturation, with a wrapping hue axis.
